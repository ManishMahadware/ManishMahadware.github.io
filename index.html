Mmanager

Your chat-first personal manager, for Android

What Mmanager does

Mmanager is an Android app built around one input box. Type a thought, speak it, or share it in from any other app, and Mmanager's AI reads it, works out what you meant, and — after you confirm — files it away automatically: as a calendar event, a task, a note, a link, or a saved file. Nothing is guessed and saved silently; you always see and approve what gets created before it's stored.

The three things it's built around

  • Smart Calendar Manager — describe a plan in plain language and Mmanager creates, updates, or reschedules the matching event in your own Google Calendar.
  • Second Brain — links, quotes, files, notes, tasks, and birthdays you capture are kept in a single searchable place on your phone, tagged and filterable.
  • Personal AI Advisor — on request, Mmanager can lay out your day, flag things going stale, and give you a short morning brief.

Where your data lives

Everything you confirm is stored in your own Google Calendar, Google Sheets, and Google Drive — Mmanager reads and writes to those on your behalf using the Google account you sign in with. A local encrypted copy is also kept on your device. See the Privacy Policy for full detail on what is accessed and how it's used.
