Privacy Policy — Mmanager

Last updated: 4 August 2026 · Developer: Manish Mahadware

Mmanager ("the app") is a chat-first personal management app for Android, published by Manish Mahadware. It helps you capture thoughts, tasks, links, files and events, and organise them into your own Google account. We collect the minimum data necessary to provide the service. This policy explains what the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

Information the app accesses

  • Google account basics — your name, email address and profile photo, used only to sign you in and show who is signed in.
  • Google Calendar (calendar scope) — the app creates, updates and deletes calendar events that you explicitly confirm (tasks, reminders, birthdays, plans). It reads events only to display your schedule inside the app and keep it in sync.
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets scope) — the app appends rows to a single "Mmanager" spreadsheet in your account, acting as your personal log of saved entries, and updates row status when entries change.
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope) — the app can only see and manage files it created itself: files you explicitly share into the app are uploaded to an "Mmanager" folder in your Drive, and optional backups are stored there. It has no access to your other Drive files.
  • Message text you type or speak — sent securely to our AI service (a Google Cloud Function using the Gemini API) solely to understand your request (e.g. turn "dentist tomorrow 5pm" into an event). Requests are rate-limited per user. Message content is processed transiently and is not used to train models.
  • Crash and usage diagnostics — anonymous crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and anonymous usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) to keep the app stable.

How your data is stored

  • Your content lives in your own Google account (Calendar, Sheets, Drive) — under your control at all times.
  • A local copy is kept on your device in an encrypted database (SQLCipher, key protected by Android Keystore). Android backup of this database is disabled.
  • We do not operate servers that store your content. Google user data is never copied to developer-controlled storage.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share Google user data with third parties, advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising or for training AI models.
  • No human at the developer's side reads your data.

Mmanager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

  • Content in your Google account (events, spreadsheet rows, Drive files) remains until you delete it — in the app or directly in Google Calendar/Sheets/Drive.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the local encrypted database.
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • "Delete account" inside the app's Settings signs you out and removes local data.

Permissions the app requests on your device

  • Microphone — only while you use voice input; audio is transcribed on-device by Android's speech service.
  • Notifications — for the optional morning brief and event reminders you set.

Children

Mmanager is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be published at this address.
